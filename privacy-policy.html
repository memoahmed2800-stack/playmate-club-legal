Playmate Club Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Playmate Club ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Playmate Club application and its related services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Playmate Club, we may collect or receive the following information:

  • Account information provided through supported authentication providers.
  • Your Playmate Club player identifier.
  • Your username.
  • Your game level and experience points.
  • Your in-game currency balance.
  • Your friends list and friend request information.
  • Your avatar configuration and customization data.
  • Game-related information required to provide multiplayer functionality.
  • Technical information required to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

2. Sign-In Services

Playmate Club may allow users to sign in using supported third-party authentication services, including Google and Apple through Unity Player Accounts.

These services may provide us with information necessary to authenticate your account and maintain your Playmate Club account. The information provided by these services is handled according to their respective privacy policies.

3. Avatar Information

Playmate Club allows users to create and customize a player avatar. Avatar configuration information may be stored and associated with the user's Playmate Club account so that the avatar can be restored when the user signs in on another supported device.

Avatar functionality may use services provided by Genies. Information handled by those services may also be subject to Genies' applicable privacy policies and terms.

4. How We Use Information

We use collected information only as necessary to provide and operate Playmate Club, including to:

  • Create and maintain player accounts.
  • Authenticate users.
  • Save usernames, levels, experience points, and in-game currency.
  • Save and restore avatar configurations.
  • Manage friends and friend requests.
  • Provide multiplayer games and matchmaking.
  • Protect the service against abuse, fraud, and unauthorized access.
  • Maintain and improve the reliability and security of the service.

5. Data Storage

Account and game information may be stored using cloud services required to operate Playmate Club. We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service or as required for legitimate operational, security, or legal purposes.

6. Third-Party Services

Playmate Club may use third-party services to provide authentication, cloud services, avatar functionality, analytics, advertising, or other necessary features.

Third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies. We do not control the privacy practices of third-party services.

7. Children's Privacy

Playmate Club is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us without appropriate authorization, please contact us so that we can review and take appropriate action.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information used by Playmate Club against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Your Choices and Data Requests

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including requesting access to, correction of, or deletion of information associated with your account.
